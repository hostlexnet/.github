Hostlex operates virtual servers, dedicated hardware, game servers and object storage in data centres across Germany and Finland. Ordering, provisioning and renewals are automated end to end: a service is delivered as soon as it is paid for and is managed from a single customer panel. When something needs a human, tickets go straight to the engineers who run the platform.

## Services

| Service | What it is | Sites |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KVM servers** | Virtual machines on AMD Ryzen 9 7950X3D and Ryzen 9 5950X hosts with DDR5 memory and NVMe storage | Frankfurt |
| **Dedicated servers** | Physical machines built to specification and quoted in writing before payment | Frankfurt |
| **Game servers** | Minecraft, Rust and Hytale on Ryzen 9 7950X3D hosts, managed through Pterodactyl | Frankfurt |
| **S3 object storage** | S3-compatible buckets, each plan bound to a single site | Falkenstein · Nuremberg · Helsinki |
| **Storage Boxes** | Fixed-size HDD storage for backups and archives | Falkenstein · Helsinki |

## How we work

- **Automation first.** Orders, provisioning and renewals run without waiting for staff.
- **Transparent billing.** The price on the website is the price at checkout. VAT is calculated for the customer's country and shown on its own line, and every payment comes with a PDF invoice.
- **Prepaid accounts.** Services are paid from the account balance, topped up by card via Stripe, bank transfer via Wise or cryptocurrency via Cryptomus.
- **Engineering support.** Tickets are free on every plan and are answered by the people who operate the infrastructure.
